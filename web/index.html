DAW SETUP ASSISTANT

Follow these steps to activate MIDI reception in your DAW

Virtual Port: VLive MIDI Controller
1
In REAPER, click Options > Preferences (or press Ctrl + P).
2
In the left sidebar, navigate to Audio > MIDI Devices.
3
In the upper list (MIDI inputs to make available to tracks...), find VLive MIDI Controller. (Notice it starts in <disabled> state).
4
Right-click on VLive MIDI Controller and select Enable input, and also check Enable input for control messages. Then click Apply.
5
Insert a Track (Ctrl + T), set Track Input to Input: MIDI > VLive MIDI Controller > All Channels, click the Record Arm (Red) button, and click Record Monitoring: ON. Click Send Test Note above — you'll immediately see the track meter light up green!
1
In Nuendo / Cubase, open Studio > Studio Setup... from the top menu bar.
2
In the left category panel, click MIDI Port Setup.
3
Locate VLive MIDI Controller under the Input Ports list. Ensure the State column shows Active.
4
Check the box in the "In 'All MIDI Inputs'" column so all instrument tracks pick it up automatically. Click OK.
5
Add an Instrument track (e.g. HALion Sonic / Retrologue). Select Input as All MIDI Inputs or VLive MIDI Controller. Hit Send Test Note to hear your synth trigger!
1
In Ableton Live, open Options > Preferences (or Ctrl + ,).
2
Select the Link / Tempo / MIDI tab on the left.
3
Scroll to MIDI Ports and find Input: VLive MIDI Controller.
4
Toggle Track: ON (to play software instruments) and Remote: ON (to MIDI map knobs, sliders, pads).
1
Press F10 in FL Studio to open Settings and switch to the MIDI tab.
2
Under Input, click on VLive MIDI Controller.
3
Click the Enable button below the list so the light turns on (orange or green).
4
Close settings. Play any selected instrument channel from your VLive stage keys or sequencer!
If your DAW was already open when VLive launched, click "Reset Devices" in your DAW preferences.
